# ACNesia — Design & UX Contract (MSC Scaffolding Industrial B2B Architecture)

## Public experience
- **Audience/job, primary action, assumptions:**
  - **Audience:** Facility managers, office procurement/GA teams, project contractors, building owners, and commercial buyers in Malang Raya, Surabaya, Sidoarjo, and East Java looking to rent standing AC units, procure new or reconditioned commercial/office AC systems, or contract vacuum-standard installation and periodic maintenance.
  - **Primary job:** Evaluate ACNesia's core HVAC capabilities (`Jual Unit Baru & Rekondisi`, `Sewa AC Standing & Proyek`, `Instalasi Vakum & Kontrak AMC`), understand the 4-step B2B execution workflow (`01 Konsultasi` → `02 Penawaran SPH Resmi` → `03 Pengiriman & Instalasi` → `04 Siap Pakai & Garansi`), inspect standard unit/service pricing (`Rp ... / unit` or `/ hari`), verify technical installation standards (`Pipa ASTM B280`, `Vakum ≤ 500 Micron`, `R32/R410A`), check workshop locations, and request an official SPH quotation (`Surat Penawaran Harga` + `e-Faktur PPN`).
  - **Primary action:** Click `Minta SPH` / `Ajukan Penawaran SPH` to open the interactive SPH & RAB Quotation Drawer (or add specific AC units/packages directly to the SPH request list).
- **Entry → decision/action → outcome; critical recovery:**
  - **Entry:** Homepage (`/`), catalog (`/produk/`), rental (`/sewa-ac/`), or service detail pages at mobile (`390px`), tablet (`768px`), or desktop (`1440px`).
  - **Decision:** Review the 3 flagship B2B service lines and 4-step workflow, compare 6 flagship SKU prices in the clean 3×2 catalog showcase, verify sector coverage and technical SOPs, or calculate room PK capacity.
  - **Action:** Add units/services to the SPH Drawer or click `Minta SPH` / `Konsultasi & Estimasi Proyek` to submit project requirements.
  - **Outcome & recovery:** Selected SPH items persist in `localStorage` across routes; form validation highlights missing fields without clearing user input; empty filter states on `/produk/` provide a one-click reset.
- **Information hierarchy and content/assets:**
  1. Dark Translucent Top Header (`rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.95)`, `80px` desktop / `72px` mobile) with brand mark (`ACNESIA` / `PT MITRA PENDINGIN NUSANTARA`), consolidated navigation (`Beranda`, `Layanan` SEO B2B dropdown, `Kalkulator PK`, `Panduan`, `Tentang`, `Kontak` — without separate `Produk AC` or `Solusi B2B` top-level menus), and pill CTA (`Minta SPH`).
  2.   3. Core Services & 4-Step Workflow Section (`bg-white`): 3 large visual service cards (`Sewa AC Standing & Event`, `Jual Unit Baru & Rekondisi`, `Instalasi & Kontrak Maintenance`) with `rounded-2xl` photos and circular `+` action buttons, followed immediately in the same section by `Alur Layanan` (`01`–`04` light slate step cards).
  4. Standard Component & Unit Catalog (`bg-white`): Header with right-aligned `Katalog Lengkap →` pill link, followed by a clean 3×2 minimal product grid (6 flagship AC & service SKUs on crisp white ground with centered title and `Rp ... / unit` or `/ hari` pricing).
  5. Project & Sector Documentation Grid (`bg-white`): 3×3 visual photo grid (`rounded-2xl` dark-gradient overlay cards with orange location/sector pill badges and white bold titles) showcasing real commercial AC use cases.
  6. ## Direction
- **Accepted direction and why it fits:**
  - **MSC Scaffolding Industrial B2B Architecture (`https://mscscaffolding.com/`):** ACNesia and MSC Scaffolding solve the exact same Indonesian B2B industrial/commercial buyer job — renting and procuring technical building equipment (AC standing/split/cassette units vs. scaffolding) with transparent per-unit/per-day pricing, strict technical installation standards (0.8mm ASTM copper pipe & 500-micron digital vacuum vs. 1.8mm SNI pipe & EN 12811), physical workshop dispatch hubs, and formal `SPH` + `e-Faktur PPN` procurement.
- **Brand/content identity; patterns intentionally repeated:**
  - Uppercase orange section kickers (`text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-widest text-orange-600`) paired with bold `Poppins` `h2` (`36px` desktop / `24px` mobile, `tracking-[-0.025em]`).
  - Pill action buttons (`rounded-full`) for primary CTAs and `rounded-2xl` (`16px`) media/content cards.
- **Responsive transformation and density:**
  - **Mobile (`390px`):** `72px` sticky dark header with brand + `Minta SPH` pill + menu button; `844px` dark hero with `30px` `h1`, pill CTA, and 2×2 bottom spec grid; 1-column service cards and 2×2 `01`–`04` workflow cards; 2-column (`grid-cols-2`) minimal product showcase matching MSC's 390px 2-column catalog; 1-column sector documentation cards; stacked dark technical SOP section; stacked workshop cards; stacked consultant portrait + estimation checklist; 1-column dark footer.
  - **Desktop (`1440px`):** `80px` sticky dark header; `900px` full-bleed dark hero with 4-column bottom spec strip; 3-column service cards + 4-column `01`–`04` workflow cards; 3×2 minimal product catalog; 3×3 sector documentation grid; 12-column split dark technical safety section; 2-column workshop facility cards; 12-column split consultant portrait + estimation section; 4-column dark footer.
- **Portfolio comparison (`scripts/portfolio-scan.py`):**
  - `portfolio-scan.py` across `/home/ongki/Projects` showed `Plus Jakarta Sans` used in 4 of the 5 recent projects (`acnesia`, `agrokomplek-cemerlang`, `agroprimagreenhouse`, `pesantren-nurul-haromain-linnisa`). Switching ACNesia to **`Poppins`** (`400`, `500`, `600`, `700`, `800`) directly matches the inspected target reference `https://mscscaffolding.com/` (`4777` `Poppins` elements in `ref.json`) and breaks cross-project font repetition.

  - `h3`: `16px` at `390px`; `18px` (`line-height 28px`) at `768px`/`1440px`, `font-bold` (`700`).

- **C5 Density / spacing rhythm:** Generous vertical section padding (`py-16 lg:py-24`) on white sections so product visuals and photography breathe cleanly without heavy card borders, contrasted against dense, authoritative `#020617` dark sections for Hero, Technical Safety Standards, and Footer.
- **C6 Signature elements:** Two-tone `h1` (white primary lines + `#fb923c` orange-400 solution line) over dark industrial photography; 4-metric technical bar anchored at the bottom of the hero with an orange progress indicator bar; borderless service cards with `rounded-2xl` photos and circular `+` button; `01`–`04` light slate workflow cards with top-right micro-icons; borderless centered 3×2 product showcase with `Rp ... / unit` pricing; 3×3 dark-gradient project cards with orange location pills; dark `#020617` K3/technical section with emerald CTA and 3 bordered compliance rows; consultant photo with floating white name/role card.
- **C7 Container boundaries:** Service cards and catalog items on `#ffffff` are open (no outer card border around the text+image group—only the image itself or subtle `bg-slate-50` workflow/depot boxes are framed), matching `mscscaffolding.com`.
- **Do not substitute & intended deviations:**
  - Do not substitute `Poppins` with `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter`.
  - Do not wrap the 3 service cards or 6 homepage catalog items in heavy bordered e-commerce boxes with multi-row variant pills on the homepage.
  - **Intended Deviation 1 (Owner WhatsApp Invariant):** `mscscaffolding.com` places WhatsApp links in the hero, warehouse cards, footer, and a mobile bottom sticky bar (`aside`). Per owner invariant (`preferences.md` line 25: *"At most ONE WhatsApp/contact call to action per page, placed after the user's task is done... NEVER in header, menu, hero, sticky bars, floating buttons, pop-ups, or repeated per list item"*), the mobile sticky bar is omitted, hero/warehouse CTAs open the interactive SPH Drawer, and the single secondary monochrome WhatsApp button is placed only in Section 7 (`Konsultasi & Estimasi Proyek`).
  - **Intended Deviation 2 (Accessible Contrast on White):** Where `mscscaffolding.com` uses `#fb923c` (`orange-400`) for kickers on dark backgrounds, we keep `#fb923c` on `#020617` dark surfaces (8.9:1 contrast) and use `#c2410c` (`orange-700`, 5.18:1 on white) / `#ea580c` with dark/white text where WCAG AA requires ≥4.5:1 for normal text.

## Tokens and behavior
- **Semantic color pairs, type, spacing, shape, elevation:**
  - Light canvas: `#ffffff` (`rgb(255, 255, 255)`) and subtle card fill `#f8fafc` (`rgb(248, 250, 252)`)
  - Dark canvas (Hero, Technical SOP, Footer): `#020617` (`rgb(2, 6, 23)`) and dark glass `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` with `border-white/10`
  - Primary ink on light: `#020617` (`text-slate-950`, 19.8:1 on white)
  - Secondary ink on light: `#475569` (`text-slate-600`, 7.5:1 on white)
  - Muted ink on dark: `#94a3b8` (`text-slate-400`, 7.1:1 on `#020617`) and `#cbd5e1` (`text-slate-300`, 12.6:1 on `#020617`)
  - Brand accent on dark: `#fb923c` (`text-orange-400`, 8.9:1 on `#020617`)
  - Brand accent on light: `#c2410c` (`text-orange-700`, 5.18:1 on `#ffffff`)
  - Primary CTA button fill: `#c2410c` (`bg-orange-700`, 5.18:1 with `#ffffff` text)
  - Technical safety accent: `#047857` (`bg-emerald-700`, 5.4:1 with `#ffffff` text) and `#34d399` (`text-emerald-400` on `#020617`)
  - Radius vocabulary: `rounded-full` (`9999px`) for buttons and badges; `rounded-2xl` (`16px`) for photo/service/workflow cards; `rounded-xl` (`12px`) for technical spec boxes
- **Theme scope; motion purpose and reduced-motion alternative:**
  - Single light canvas with intentional dark `#020617` architectural sections (Hero, K3/Technical SOP, Footer); `150ms` hover transitions; respects `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- **Relevant loading/empty/error/success/disabled states:**
  - Interactive SPH & Estimasi Drawer (`RfqDrawer`) and quick PK calculator preserve user inputs in `localStorage`, validate required fields inline, and display clear empty/confirmation states.
- **Framework-native implementation and budget:**
  - Astro 5 static prerendered pages + Google Fonts `Poppins` + native DOM scripts for SPH Drawer and interactive PK calculator + Preact islands on `/produk/` catalog filter.
